# Funding Opportunities to Verify

This is a lead list, not a confirmation of eligibility, current availability, or an application. Check the official source and record the date checked before relying on any program.

| Opportunity | Potential fit | Key checks | Official link |
|---|---|---|---|
| New York State Digital Game Development Program | Qualifying digital game production expenses performed in New York | Current application window, minimum spend, eligible production costs, location rules, applicant requirements, certification timing, and tax-credit mechanics | https://esd.ny.gov/new-york-state-digital-game-development-program |
| Epic MegaGrants | A compelling Unreal Engine project or qualifying real-time 3D technology | Whether applications are open, current rules, required engine use, project maturity, and deliverables | https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/megagrants |
| Unity for Humanity | A Unity-based project with measurable social impact | Next application round, use of Unity, prototype/demo requirements, impact criteria, and current terms | https://unity.com/humanity |
| National Science Foundation | Genuine research and development that fits a specific solicitation | Applicant eligibility, solicitation scope, research merit, allowable costs, and deadlines | https://www.nsf.gov/funding |
| Federal SBIR/STTR programs | Eligible R&D aligned with a participating agency's needs | Current authorization and solicitations, eligible small-business structure, ownership/control, technical novelty, and agency fit | https://www.sbir.gov/ |
| Crowdfunding | Clearly bounded prototype or first-game milestone | Audience demand, reward costs, platform fees, delivery risk, tax/accounting treatment, and truthful claims | https://www.kickstarter.com/ |
| Sponsorships and paid pilots | Demonstrable educational, accessibility, creator, or community use case | Named partner, written scope, measurable outcomes, procurement rules, and support obligations | Find partners directly; no program assumed |

## Priority
1. Validate the New York tax-credit route with the state and a qualified accountant; do not treat it as upfront grant cash.
2. Pick either a real-time 3D game demo or a research-led prototype and match it to the relevant current grant criteria.
3. Build an audience and collect evidence before crowdfunding.
4. Approach pilot partners with a demo and a bounded proposal, not an unfunded promise of the full platform.
