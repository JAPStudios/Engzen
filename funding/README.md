# Engzen Funding Package

This folder contains the first working funding package for Engzen and Jelly & Peanut Studios. It is a planning package, not a submitted application, award, legal opinion, or promise of eligibility.

## Included
- [Funding plan](funding-plan.md): staged fundraising strategy and milestones.
- [Prototype budget](prototype-budget.csv): editable planning estimates in USD.
- [Funding opportunities](funding-opportunities.md): target programs and eligibility checks.
- [Application tracker](application-tracker.csv): status tracker for outreach and applications.

## Immediate objective
Fund a bounded prototype phase, not the entire long-term platform at once. The prototype should demonstrate a reproducible project workflow, a small playable sample, asset import/modeling workflow, scripting, packaging, and public documentation.

## Important safeguards
1. Confirm the legal applicant, tax status, ownership of intellectual property, and grant eligibility before applying.
2. Do not treat a tax credit as upfront cash. Confirm qualifying expenses, application timing, minimum spend, location rules, and whether a project must be approved before costs are incurred.
3. Do not claim a feature is implemented until there is a working build and evidence.
4. Obtain written permission before using third-party game names, logos, artwork, or assets in marketing materials.
5. Keep restricted grant funds and records separate as required by the award.
6. Estimates are placeholders, not vendor quotes or commitments. Replace them with real quotes, actual rates, and a documented work plan.
